The full name for this game will probably be something like "Future // Kevin".

THe game is based around the NFT of Kevin that went viral in 2021 for being so funny looking. I've made a meshy 3d rendering of him and given him some movements in mixamo. 

The game is really an excuse to showcase the song I made about him when he was going viral.

The gameplay is basically an endless runner, with the twist that Kevin is running TOWARDS the screen (so we can see him, he's Kevin!). The way I see it in my head is that he is running through a (jungle?) futuristic landscape with Cryptopunks trying to attack him and he has to punch them. The gameplay will revolve around the direction from which the Cryptopunks are attacking - they'll come from the left, right and center, and the player has to hit the right button to trigger the correct punch or kick or whatever to kill them.

If we have time we'll add other obstacles, jumping, mabye powerups etc.
